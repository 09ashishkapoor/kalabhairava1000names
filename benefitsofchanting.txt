Benefits of Chanting 
Awakens Guru Tatva that is latent within all living beings. 
Awakens Baba's ever-present companionship in your life. 
Updates your firmware and awakens Shakti Vidya in the jivah. 
What else do you need ? Ask the Kaashikaa-Pura-Adhinaatha,Kalabhairava Baba.
These 1000 names are sufficient to attain whatever you want to do in the path of Dharma.


How to Chant
Just read the names. Stutter, fumble ,sing like a song, with OM or without it. 
Pronounciation will get better as you read this often.
One at a time, 11 a time or 1000 in one sitting. 
Understand the meanings or not, it doesnt matter. 
There is no wrong way to approach Bhairav baba. 
Just like poison kills you if you know something contains it or not. 
These 1000 names of bhairav baba act like poison for your fears and he makes you fearless in the path of Dharma. 
Listen to audio version. there are plenty on Youtube.
Ma Krishna the Guru of KaliYuga has already decreed "Nama Japa" is sufficient so listen to him.
